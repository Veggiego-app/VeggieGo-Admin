Delete Your VeggieGo Account

If you want to delete your VeggieGo account and associated data, please send an email to:

support@veggiego.co.in

Include your registered phone number in the email.

Data Deleted:

  • Name
  • Phone Number
  • Saved Addresses
  • Order History
  • Account Information

Retention Period

Account deletion requests are processed within 7 days. Some transaction records may be retained where required by law.
